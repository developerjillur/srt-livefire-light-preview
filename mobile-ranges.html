Overview

Mobile Range

We’ll even get you the “Mack Truck” to haul it if you like.

SRT’s Mobile Range is a completely self-contained, truly mobile live-fire training platform providing a 10m shooting distance; perfect for on-location / multiple-location live-fire judgmental training or other qualification and practice.

The Mobile Range is housed in a standard DOT-approved 53’ semi-trailer and includes a bullet trap, true 360-degree positive ballistic containment (ZERO-SDZ), OSHA/NIOSH/EPA/NEHAC/NAVFAC compliant ventilation system with HEPA filtration, bullet-proof control room/operator’s station, anti-ricochet treatment, interior and exterior acoustic treatments, lighting, communications, standard shore-power hookup, stow-able stairs, leveling jacks and Thermal Shot virtual target system by Laser Shot.

Options include an onboard generator, overhead monorail targets, shooting dividers, a CCTV system with a remote monitor, and custom paint and graphics packages for the exterior. We’ll even get you the “Mack Truck” to haul it if you like.

Thermal Shot™ / Live-fire simulator

Judgmental training, on location

The exclusive Thermal Shot™ live-fire simulator system ships as a standard feature. Live rounds pass through the projected scenario, the thermal camera registers every strike, and shooters run judgmental scenarios at the 10 metre line — in a trailer parked at their own facility.
