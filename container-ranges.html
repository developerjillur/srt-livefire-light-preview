Overview

Container ranges

Connect them end-to-end and a drop-in unit becomes a 100 metre range.

SRT installs the latest shooting range technology in a compact self-contained unit through the use of modified 40’ shipping containers, which can be connected end-to-end for a maximum shooting distance of 100 meters. These ranges are in use by firearm manufacturers who need a testing range as well as police and military units who have frequent training and qualification needs.

Optionally, Laser Shot’s powerful Thermal Shot™ technology can transform each Container Range into a virtual training center featuring challenging software with moving targets at simulated distances.
